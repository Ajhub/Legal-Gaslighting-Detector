Legal Gaslighting Detector exposes the gap between what corporate and 
government statements legally imply — and what they actually commit to.

Paste any privacy policy, AI disclosure, press release, or terms of 
service. The tool identifies every legal claim in the text, classifies 
it, and explains in plain English what it really means under real law.

Each claim is scored across six types:
  - Empty      → zero legal content, unverifiable by design
  - Misleading → true under a narrow definition, false under relevant law  
  - Weasel     → undefined terms with no legal backing
  - Audit      → audit paid for by the subject, no legal standard
  - Substantive→ cites a specific law with real obligations
  - Omission   → conspicuous absence of a key legal right
